HAIR STYLING

Hair styling for women encompasses a wide array of techniques and styles aimed at enhancing appearance and expressing personality. From simple trims to elaborate updos, hair styling allows women to personalize their look and boost their confidence.

HIGHLIGHT

Hair highlights are a coloring technique where strands of hair are lightened to create dimension and brightness. They can enhance your natural hair color or provide a bold new look without a full color change. Highlights are versatile and can be tailored to various hair types and preferences, from subtle babylights to bold chunky highlights.

PEDICURE

A pedicure is a cosmetic treatment for the feet and toenails, similar to a manicure but focused on the lower extremities. It involves various steps like soaking, scrubbing, cuticle care, nail shaping, and often, nail polish application. Pedicures can also include foot massages and other treatments like scrubs or masks, aiming to improve both the appearance and health of the feet.

MANICURE

A manicure is a mostly cosmetic beauty treatment for the fingernails and hands performed at home or in a nail salon. A manicure usually consists of filing and shaping the free edge of nails, pushing and clipping (with a cuticle pusher and cuticle nippers) any nonliving tissue at the cuticle and removing hangnails, treatments with various liquids, massage of the hand, and the application of fingernail polish.
